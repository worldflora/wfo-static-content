> *Uniting global expertise to document and understand the extraordinary diversity of Rubiaceae*

Rubiaceae is one of the largest families of flowering plants and a prominent component of natural vegetation worldwide, commonly known as the coffee family. It contains about 14,200 species across 615 genera and has a cosmopolitan distribution, but is most diverse in tropical and subtropical regions. 

The Rubiaceae Taxonomic Expert Network brings together an international group of specialists to coordinate and advance taxonomic, systematic, and nomenclatural research in the family. Its primary aim is to develop and maintain a stable, community-endorsed taxonomic backbone for Rubiaceae within the World Flora Online. By integrating evidence from morphology, molecular phylogenetics, nomenclature, and regional floristic expertise, the Rubiaceae TEN seeks to improve data quality, reduce duplication of effort, and support downstream research in biodiversity, ecology, and conservation.

This is an ongoing project, and we continue to welcome collaborators to help with the taxonomic curation of the family, and supply or produce descriptive content to populate the World Flora Online portal. If you would like to participate in this endeavour and share your taxonomic expertise, please get in touch.

### Contributors

The Rubiaceae Taxonomic Expert Group is composed of specialists from around the world, representing a wide range of institutions and research centers. The group brings together expertise in taxonomy, systematics, nomenclature, phylogenetics, floristics, and related fields, ensuring comprehensive and up-to-date treatment of the family.
