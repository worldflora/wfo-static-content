## Data access & APIs

Our mission is to collate data from multiple sources and make it available for everyone to use. 
The main way we do this is through this website but we also offer access via periodic downloads 
and APIs. __Please do not scrape large amounts of data from our websites and APIs.__ 
If you need data in bulk and it isn't already available as a prepackaged download 
please ask us and we will help you get it in the form you need.

The [WFO technical documentation](https://plant-list-docs.rbge.info/) is available to read
if you need background on how we curate and publish the data.

### Taxonomic and nomenclatural data

The WFO Plant List forms the taxonomic backbone of this website.
