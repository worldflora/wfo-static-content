## Jardin Botanico Nacional de Cuba

The National Botanical Garden is located south of Havana, about 22 km from its center. It is an educational, scientific, and recreational institution belonging to the University of Havana, serving as an exhibition center for Cuban and tropical plant life, boasting 4,000 species across approximately 600 hectares.

### Mission
The National Botanical Garden, founded in 1968, is a university institution that coordinates the National Network of Botanical Gardens, is a leader in the study and writing of the work "Flora of the Republic of Cuba", provides environmental education to its visitors and contributes to the scientific research and conservation of plants and fungi, as well as to the teaching of Botany at the university level.

### Vision
An institution comprised of multiple stakeholders, it is a leader in the National Network of Botanical Gardens, environmental education, and university-level botany instruction. We contribute to the research and conservation of Cuban plants and fungi. Our living plant collections serve as the primary setting for education and recreation, reflecting our social commitment and ensuring sustainability.

Location: El Rocío street Km 3 1/2, ZC 19230, Calabazar, Boyeros, Havana.
