# WFO Taxonomic Backbone curation -- the 'Wish List' (2025)

This is the list of plant Families or groups with more than about 500 accepted species for which we do not have Taxonomic Expert Networks (TENs), as of September 2025. We are particularly keen to hear from people who would be interested in forming a TEN for any of these groups.
